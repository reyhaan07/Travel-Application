## How to take a screenshot on Windows
- **Whole screen:** press `PrtScn`, then paste into Paint and save here.
- **One window:** click the window, press `Alt + PrtScn`, paste and save.
- **Snip a region:** press `Windows + Shift + S`, drag a box, then paste and save.
